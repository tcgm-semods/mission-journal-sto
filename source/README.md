# Mission Journal (STO-Style)

Replaces the Mission Log window (Menu &rarr; Missions, or the **J** hotkey)
with a layout closer to Star Trek Online's mission journal:

- **Category sidebar** &mdash; your missions are grouped by category instead
  of a flat list. Missions ready to turn in float to their own "Ready to
  Claim" group at the top. Click a category header to collapse/expand it.
- **Persistent detail pane** &mdash; click a mission in the sidebar and its
  description, objectives, and rewards appear right there, no popup. Claim,
  Abandon, and Autopilot buttons live in the same pane.
- **Trackable mission** &mdash; click the star on a mission's detail pane to
  track it. A small HUD widget in the top-left then shows that mission's
  current objective at all times, even with the journal closed. Click the
  widget's &times; to untrack. Tracked mission persists across sessions.

The Ongoing/Completed switch, search box, and close button all still work
as before &mdash; only the layout changed. Station-offered mission
Accept/Refuse and the mission-complete story popup are untouched.
